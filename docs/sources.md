# Sources

Every number used in the v0.2 design, grouped by what it supports. Sources
marked **low quality** are context only and are not cited in reports.

## Histamine timing and sample type

- Bhat KN et al. Plasma histamine changes during provoked bronchospasm in asthmatic patients. *J Allergy Clin Immunol* 1976;58:647-56. Plasma histamine rose within 5 min of inhaled allergen (1.4 to 3.5 ng/mL), 1.9 by 30 min; no rise with methacholine.
- Busse WW, Swenson CA. *J Allergy Clin Immunol* 1989;84:658-66. Plasma histamine 73 to 1071 pg/mL, peak ~5 min after allergen, baseline by 30 min.
- Heavey DJ et al. Histamine released locally after intradermal antigen challenge in man. *Br J Clin Pharmacol* 1984;18:915-9. Local peak 2-15 min.
- Ohtsuka T et al. *Ann Allergy* 1993;71:139-46. After food challenge, histamine raised at 2 and 4 h, tryptase at 4 h.
- Miliani H et al. Twenty-four-hour rhythmic variations of histamine in human female blood. *Biol Rhythm Res* 2000;31:136-45. 24 h rhythm; whole blood ~1000x plasma.
- Bugalho de Almeida AA et al. *Klin Wochenschr* 1982;60:673-80. >=80% of whole-blood histamine in basophils.
- Christ P et al. The circadian clock drives mast cell functions in allergic reactions. *Front Immunol* 2018;9:1526. Histamine and tryptase peak at night.
- Wagner A et al. *Adv Dermatol Allergol* 2019;36:538-43. Lower DAO, higher blood histamine in allergic patients.
- Lamkin Clinic, Whole blood histamine reference page. **Low quality** (functional medicine clinic).

## S1P

- Kowal K et al. Altered sphingolipid metabolism is associated with asthma phenotype in house dust mite-allergic patients. *Allergy Asthma Immunol Res* 2019;11:330-42. S1P 168 to 215 pmol/mL at 45 min in dual responders; tracks late-response severity (r = 0.60).
- Ammit AJ et al. Sphingosine 1-phosphate modulates human airway smooth muscle cell functions that promote inflammation and airway remodeling in asthma. *FASEB J* 2001;15:1212-4.
- Roviezzo F et al. S1P-induced airway smooth muscle hyperresponsiveness and lung inflammation in vivo. *Br J Pharmacol* 2015;172:1882-93.
- Baranowski M et al. Exercise increases plasma levels of sphingoid base-1 phosphates in humans. *Acta Physiol* 2011;203:373-80 (cited via Kowal 2019).
- Grasso et al. *Anal Bioanal Chem* 2023;415:6573-82 and Seidita et al. *iScience* 2024;27:111168. Lab S1P methods; no point-of-care test.

## Sensor platform

- Wyss Institute. abbieSense anaphylaxis diagnostic; Overhauling diagnostics for better patient care (2019). Histamine from a drop of blood in ~5-10 min; coating keeps >80% sensitivity after a month in plasma.
- Timilsina SS et al. Enabling multiplexed electrochemical detection of biomarkers with high sensitivity in complex biological samples. *Acc Chem Res* 2021;54:3529-39. Control electrode, no cross-signal between electrodes, single-digit pg/mL from 15 uL.
- Wyss Institute. eRapid: developing a multiplexed electrochemical diagnostic platform from the ground up (2021).
- Mairal-Lerga et al. *J Hazard Mater* 2025;494:138540. Aptamer strip test for histamine, 2 uL finger prick, <10 min, LOD ~1 ng/mL.

## Wearables

- Huffaker MF et al. Passive nocturnal physiologic monitoring enables early detection of exacerbations in children with asthma. *AJRCCM* 2018;198:320-8. 47% sensitivity, 96% specificity.
- Apple Support. How to use the Blood Oxygen app on Apple Watch; Apple Newsroom, An update on Blood Oxygen for Apple Watch in the US (Aug 2025).
- Predictor Solutions blog, Wearables and remote monitoring (2026). **Low quality** (vendor blog).

## Adherence and self-testing

- Brummer LE et al. *eClinicalMedicine* 2026;94:103838. COVID self-test uptake 57%, schedule adherence 76% (88% one-off, 53% twice weekly).
- Herbert C et al. *JMIR Form Res* 2022;6:e38113. 35.9% highly adherent over 12 weeks.
- Fisher KA et al. *JAMA Netw Open* 2025 (via MedPage Today). 30% of US adults would not or might not self-test.
- Bien-Gund C et al. *JAMA Netw Open* 2021;4:e2034001. 86% motivated to self-test.
- Borek AJ et al. *BMC Public Health* 2025;25:695. 71-100% find self-testing acceptable; blood sampling harder.
- Smith IP et al. *BMJ Open Diabetes Res Care* 2023;11:e003025. Finger-prick adherence 20-88%; cost 5x more important than other device features.
- Adherence to self-monitoring of blood glucose, Al-Ahsa primary care study (Saudi Arabia). 14% highly adherent; finger pain a barrier.
